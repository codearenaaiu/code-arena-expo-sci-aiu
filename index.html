MISSION OVERVIEW

Code Arena & Expo 2026 is organized by the School of Computing and Informatics (SCI) Committee 2025/2026 at Albukhary International University. Designed as a high-stakes, elimination-style tournament, it challenges programmers across Python, C, and Java in progressive difficulty brackets, while transforming the campus into an active innovation expo with live auditorium feeds.
